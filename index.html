About Us

A global, diversified
alternative investment
firm with a mission.

Catalyst Funds Management is focused on growing and protecting capital through various investment strategies which produce returns that are stable and independent of global stock market performance. Founded by a senior leadership team that is ex Goldman Sachs with decades of experience.

The team not only have a passion for investing in global markets, but also dedication in building relationships with our handpicked clients. We are all about people, long-term relationships, and trust.

We are deeply passionate about building and protecting a culture of curiosity, collaboration and long-term thinking. We hold ourselves to the highest standards of performance and ethics.

01
Seek the Truth
The best ideas win
on merit.

We pursue the truth with rigour and independent thinking — not hierarchy, bias, or consensus. We question deeply, think clearly and leave no stone unturned in our search for clarity and conviction.

02
One Team
Collective intelligence
over individual brilliance.

We work as one team — building trust, collaborating openly and holding ourselves and each other accountable to deliver exceptional outcomes that no one could achieve alone.

03
Think Long Term
Enduring outcomes
over short-term gains.

We think and act for the long term. We prioritise enduring outcomes, applying discipline, tenacity and patience to achieve results that compound meaningfully over time.

04
Integrity
Trust built through
every action.

We act with honesty, accountability and respect in every decision and interaction — especially when it is difficult. We build trust through our actions and safeguard our reputation relentlessly.
